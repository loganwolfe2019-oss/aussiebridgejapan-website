Our Services

What We Do for Your Brand in Japan

From compliance paperwork to introductions with Japanese buyers: the work it takes to get an Australian brand selling in Japan.

01

Brand Representation

Act as the official Japanese representative for your brand, managing all local relationships and strategic positioning.

  • Brand positioning for Japanese consumers
  • Local brand management
  • Trade show representation
  • PR & media coordination
02

Compliance Coordination

We coordinate all regulatory compliance and documentation for seamless entry into Japan. We help you prepare Japanese labelling before anything ships.

  • Regulatory compliance & documentation
  • Help preparing Japanese labelling before anything ships
  • Quarantine & food safety guidance
  • Cold chain logistics coordination
03

Import & Storage

We start in Nagoya. Your stock is imported and stored in Japan, and we introduce it to venues and shops there first. Import and storage details are confirmed per brand.

  • Freight forwarder and customs broker organised for you
  • Stock stored in Japan, starting in Nagoya
  • Delivery to the venues and shops that order
  • Other cities as demand for your brand grows
04

Market Entry Strategy

Launch planning, competitive analysis, and long-term growth consulting tailored to the Japanese market.

  • Market research & feasibility
  • Competitive analysis
  • Pricing strategy for Japan
  • Growth roadmap development
05

Retail

Pitch for shelf space with specialty retailers, supermarkets and department store food halls.

  • Department store food halls (Depachika)
  • Supermarket chains
  • Specialty importers
  • Online retail platforms
06

Hospitality

Introduce your products to bars, restaurants and hotels, starting in Nagoya.

  • High-end bars & cocktail lounges
  • Premium restaurants
  • International hotel chains
  • Private members clubs

Our Commercial Model

A Partnership, Not a Purchase

We work on consignment. Your stock remains yours until it is sold in Japan; rather than buying it, we represent and sell it on your behalf.

Our fees combine a charge for storage, handling and representation with a commission on sales. Terms are agreed individually with each brand.

A

Storage, Handling & Representation

Keeping your stock in Japan, moving it to the venues and shops that order, and representing your brand to buyers.

B

Commission on Sales

A share of each sale, so our success is tied directly to yours.

We are happy to prepare an indicative proposal after an initial conversation.
