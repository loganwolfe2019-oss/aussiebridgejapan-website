Consignment

Sell in Japan on Consignment, Without Giving Up Your Stock

Many routes into Japan begin with a distributor buying your stock at a wholesale price. We work differently: your stock stays yours until it is sold in Japan, and we represent it to Japanese venues and shops on your behalf.

Two Routes

Selling to a Distributor, or Consignment

Selling to a distributor

  • The distributor buys your stock at a wholesale price, and it becomes theirs.
  • How, where and at what price it is sold is largely their decision.
  • You are paid for each order they place with you.
  • Your brand sits alongside the others in their portfolio.

Consignment with Aussie Bridge Japan

  • Your stock stays yours until it is sold in Japan.
  • We represent your brand to bars, restaurants and shops, starting in Nagoya.
  • You are paid as your stock sells, with a statement showing what sold.
  • Our fees include a commission on sales, so our success is tied directly to yours.
How It Works

From Your Warehouse to a Japanese Bar Counter

01

We agree the plan

On a first call we talk through your range and whether Japan suits it. If it does, we prepare an indicative proposal and agree terms with you.

02

Your stock ships to Japan

You remain the exporter. We organise the freight forwarder and customs broker, and your goods are cleared through Japanese customs by an importer we arrange.

03

Stored in Nagoya, sold to venues

Your stock is stored in Nagoya and delivered to the bars, restaurants and shops that order it, while we represent your brand to buyers.

04

You are paid as it sells

Sales are made in yen and paid out to you in Australian dollars, with a statement showing what sold.

A Good Fit?

Is Consignment Right for Your Brand?

It tends to suit brands that

  • Want to keep ownership of their stock and a say in how their brand is presented.
  • Are ready to fund a first shipment and the Japanese labelling it needs.
  • Can allow time for orders to build, as Japan rewards patience.

It may not suit you if

  • You need full payment for your stock before it leaves Australia.
  • You are looking for a one-off bulk sale rather than building your brand in Japan.
Questions

Consignment Questions

Who owns the stock?

You do. Your stock stays yours until it is sold in Japan. Rather than buying it, we represent and sell it on your behalf.

Who pays for freight and import costs?

Your brand does. We organise the freight forwarder and customs broker, who invoice you directly, and Japan-side duty and import costs are passed on at actual cost. Arrangements are confirmed for each brand before anything ships.

How and when are we paid?

As your stock sells. Sales are made in yen and paid out to you in Australian dollars, with a statement showing what sold.

What does it cost?

A fee for storage, handling and representation, plus a commission on sales. Terms are agreed individually with each brand, and we are happy to prepare an indicative proposal after a first conversation.

What happens to stock that does not sell?

It remains yours. We review sales with you regularly, and if a product is not moving we agree the next step together, such as a clearance price, returning it, or disposing of it.

Do we need a company in Japan?

No. Your goods are cleared through Japanese customs by an importer we arrange, and we look after storage, delivery and representation in Japan.
