Questions

Common Questions

Straight answers to what Australian brands ask us most.

Do you buy our stock?

No. We work on consignment: your stock stays yours until it is sold in Japan. Rather than buying it, we represent and sell it on your behalf.

What does it cost?

A fee for storage, handling and representation, plus a commission on sales. Terms are agreed individually with each brand, and we are happy to prepare an indicative proposal after a first conversation.

Who pays for freight and import costs?

Your brand does. We organise the freight forwarder and customs broker, who invoice you directly, and Japanese duty and import costs are passed on at actual cost.

Who imports the product into Japan?

A licensed Japanese importer clears your goods through customs on consignment, so ownership stays with you. Your stock is then stored in Nagoya. Import arrangements are confirmed for each brand.

Do we need to send a full container?

No. We usually suggest a smaller first shipment to test the market, then larger volumes on an agreed schedule as sales build.

Does our packaging need Japanese labels?

Yes. Products sold in Japan need Japanese labelling. We will guide you on what your product needs before anything ships.

How long does it take to see sales?

Japan rewards patience. The first months usually go on paperwork, labelling and pricing, followed by tastings with buyers. Regular orders take time to build, and no one can guarantee results.

Where in Japan do you start?

In Nagoya. We introduce your products to bars, restaurants and shops there first, and look to other cities as demand for your brand grows.

What happens on the free consultation?

It is a 15-minute call. We will ask about your products, pricing and volumes, and give you an honest view of whether Japan suits your brand. There is no obligation.
